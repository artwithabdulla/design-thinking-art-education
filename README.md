Design Thinking Art Education

A collection of STEM-integrated visual arts and graphic design classroom resources focused on creativity, design thinking, project-based learning, and interdisciplinary problem solving.

About

I am a high school Visual Arts and Graphic Design educator with 15+ years of experience developing creative, technology-integrated learning systems for students. My courses combine studio art, digital media, design thinking, and collaborative problem solving within a STEM-focused environment.

This repository is intended to organize and share educational resources, classroom workflows, project frameworks, and instructional systems that support accessible and real-world arts education.

Educational Focus Areas

- Visual Arts Curriculum Design
- Graphic Design & Digital Media
- Design Thinking & STEM Integration
- Project-Based Learning (PBL)
- Creative Workflow Systems
- Student Reflection & Portfolio Development
- AI Integration in Arts Education
- Visual Communication & Creative Problem Solving

STEM Design Thinking Process

The instructional systems and projects in this repository are aligned with a STEM-centered build and reflection process:

1. Ask
2. Research
3. Imagine
4. Plan
5. Create
6. Test
7. Iterate
8. Reflect

Students use this framework to develop creative confidence, critical thinking skills, and collaborative problem-solving abilities through both traditional and digital media.
